# Acme New Hire Onboarding Checklist

This checklist is for new employees and their managers. People Operations sends it with the offer
letter, and the IT portal tracks the IT steps for you.

## Before Your First Day

IT ships your MacBook Pro to arrive at least 5 business days before your start date. Your Okta,
Google Workspace and Slack accounts are created 2 business days before you start, and the sign-in
link is sent to your personal email.

Your manager assigns an onboarding buddy from your team and books a 30-minute welcome call for your
first morning.

## Day One

- Sign in to Okta and set up Okta Verify for multi-factor authentication.
- Install GlobalProtect from Self Service if your role uses internal tools.
- Complete your Workday profile: address, emergency contact and bank details for payroll.
- US employees complete Form I-9 with People Operations by the end of the third day.
- Join #general, #it-help and your team channel in Slack.

## First Week

- Complete security awareness training. It takes about 45 minutes and is due within 7 days; access
  to customer data stays locked until it is done.
- Read and acknowledge the Employee Handbook and the Code of Conduct in Workday.
- Meet your onboarding buddy at least twice.
- Request any extra equipment, such as a monitor, through the IT portal.

## First 30 Days

- Choose your benefits in Workday within 30 days of your start date, or you are enrolled in the
  Acme PPO for yourself only.
- Review your 401(k): you are enrolled automatically at 3 percent after 30 days unless you choose
  another rate.
- Agree on 90-day goals with your manager in your 30-day check-in.

## First 90 Days

Your manager runs a 90-day review covering your goals, feedback from your buddy and team, and what
support you need next. Your first full performance review is in the next April or October cycle.
