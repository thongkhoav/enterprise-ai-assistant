# Billing And Contracts FAQ

This FAQ supports finance operations, support agents, and customer success managers when answering
enterprise billing and contract questions. It does not replace legal review for negotiated contract
terms.

## Invoice Corrections

Invoice corrections require invoice ID, customer ID, billing contact, correction reason, original
amount, corrected amount, tax impact, and approver. Corrections below 1,000 USD may be approved by
finance operations. Corrections from 1,000 to 10,000 USD require finance manager approval.
Corrections above 10,000 USD require finance director approval.

If an invoice correction is caused by a product defect, the ticket should reference the defect ID or
incident ID. Finance uses that reference to report defect-related billing impact.

## Credits

Service credits are different from refunds. A credit applies to a future invoice. A refund returns
money to the customer. Support agents may explain the difference but may not promise either one
without finance approval.

Service credits related to SLA misses require evidence of the SLA target, measured performance,
affected period, customer impact, and contract clause. Credits related to goodwill require account
owner approval and finance operations approval.

## Refund Eligibility

Refund eligibility depends on plan, contract terms, purchase date, service usage, and whether the
request is related to a confirmed Acme error. Standard refund processing takes 5 to 10 business days
after approval. Refund requests must be acknowledged within 24 business hours.

Strategic customers may request expedited handling through their customer success manager. Expedited
handling does not guarantee approval; it only changes review priority.

## Payment Failures

Payment failure tickets should include invoice ID, payment method type, failure timestamp, processor
error code, customer billing contact, and whether access is currently affected. Support should not
request full card numbers or bank credentials.

If a payment failure affects customer access, support should check whether the account has a grace
period, whether the failure is caused by processor outage, and whether finance operations approved a
temporary extension.

## Contract Questions

Questions about indemnity, data residency commitments, limitation of liability, custom SLA terms,
security addenda, and termination rights must be escalated to legal operations. Support may provide
standard documentation links but should not interpret custom contract language.

If the customer references a contract clause, the support agent should ask for the clause number and
route the question to legal operations with account owner copied.

## Billing Communication

Billing communication should state what happened, what amount is affected, what approval is needed,
what the expected timeline is, and what the customer should do next. Do not blame the customer,
payment processor, or internal teams. Use neutral language such as "the payment attempt was
declined" or "the invoice requires correction."
