Benefits 2026

Health coverage starts on your first day. Here is a summary of what Acme offers employees in the United States; the Benefits Guide has the details.

Open enrollment: November 3 to 17. Choose your 2027 medical plan and HSA in Workday. Outside this window you can only change plans after a qualifying life event.
Medical

Acme PPO or Acme HDHP

90%

of your premium is paid by Acme, and 75% for dependents. Preventive care is free on both plans.

Retirement

401(k) with Fidelity

4% match

Acme matches 100% of what you put in, up to 4% of base salary. The match vests immediately.

Wellbeing

Wellness stipend and EAP

$75 / month

For gyms, classes or apps, paid through payroll. Lyra counseling is free and confidential.

Savings

Health savings account

$750 – $1,500

Acme's yearly HSA contribution on the HDHP, for individual and family coverage.
